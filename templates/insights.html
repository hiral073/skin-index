  • Loves, reviews and ratings show shopper engagement on one retailer's website. They are not sales figures.

  • Ratings with few reviews are ignored: the well-reviewed list needs at least {{ config.well_reviewed_min_reviews }} reviews and the price bands need at least {{ config.band_min_reviews }}. Brand averages are weighted by review count.
